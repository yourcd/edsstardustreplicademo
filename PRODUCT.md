# PRODUCT — India Uncharted (descriptive, preserve mode)

> Same-design migration. This is a **descriptive** snapshot of the existing
> site, not an authored redesign brief. Every value traces to captured content
> or a lifted CSS value; nothing here is invented.

## Register

`brand` — a marketing/brochure travel site whose job is to inspire trips and
capture leads (enquiry forms, WhatsApp, phone CTAs throughout).

## Product Purpose

India Uncharted is a bespoke India travel operator. The site sells curated and
customizable tour packages — Rajasthan wildlife, Golden Triangle, Goa yoga &
wellness retreats, Kashmir honeymoons, motorcycle expeditions, yoga teacher
training — organized by destination and by theme, with per-package detail pages,
destination guides, an activity taxonomy, and a travel blog. Primary conversion
is an enquiry ("Get in touch" / "Send enquiry" / WhatsApp / call).

## Users

Leisure travelers planning trips to India: families, honeymooners, wellness/yoga
seekers, adventure and motorcycle travelers, student/educational groups. Copy is
warm, reassuring, and experience-led ("Turning Vacations Into Lifelong Stories").

## Brand Personality

Warm, earthy, editorial, boutique. A serif display face (Bodoni Moda) over a
clean sans body (Poppins) reads as premium-but-approachable. Palette is warm
neutral — soft brown, coral accent, linen surfaces — evoking heritage and
craft rather than a loud OTA/discount aesthetic.

## Anti-references

_provenance: inferred — the visual system reads as deliberately away from a
mass-market OTA (no dense fare tables, no neon discount chrome); coral is the
single saturated accent on an otherwise warm-neutral canvas.

## Design Principles

Descriptive, derived from the shipped artifact:

- **Warm-neutral canvas, single coral accent.** Backgrounds are white/linen;
  coral (`#E3876E`) is reserved for CTAs, active states, and highlights.
- **Serif display, sans body.** Bodoni Moda for headings/eyebrows, Poppins for
  running text and UI.
- **Rounded, card-based composition.** 20px card radii, pill buttons (100px),
  soft brown-tinted shadows; destination/package/blog content is presented as
  image cards on a light canvas.
- **Section rhythm.** Centered section titles with a short lede, generous
  vertical padding, a contained ~1342px max width.
