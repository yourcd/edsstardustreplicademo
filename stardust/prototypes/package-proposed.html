Goa Yoga Retreat Package (3 Days / 2 Nights)

If you’re looking to combine relaxation with light wellness, this Goa Yoga Retreat offers a peaceful balance of beach time, guided yoga sessions, and slow travel. It’s ideal for unwinding rather than intense spiritual practice.

Tour Overview

Duration: 2 Nights / 3 Days
Destination Covered: North or South Goa
Pickup & Drop: Goa Airport / Railway Station
Tour Type: Private / Small Group Retreat
Ideal For: Beginners, couples, solo travelers

Tour Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Beachside Relaxation

Arrive in Goa and transfer to your yoga retreat or beach resort.

  • Welcome drink & check-in
  • Evening guided yoga session (stretch & relaxation)
  • Leisure walk on the beach
  • Dinner & overnight stay

Day 2: Yoga, Meditation & Exploration

Start your day with wellness activities.

  • Morning yoga & pranayama session by the beach
  • Healthy breakfast
  • Optional activities:
    • Visit nearby beaches (Palolem / Baga / Ashwem)
    • Ayurvedic massage (optional)
  • Sunset meditation session
  • Dinner & overnight stay

Day 3: Departure

  • Morning light yoga session
  • Breakfast
  • Check-out & drop

Package Inclusions

  • 2 Nights stay in a beach resort / yoga retreat
  • Daily breakfast & dinner
  • 2 yoga sessions + 1 meditation session
  • Airport/station transfers
  • Basic wellness guidance

Package Exclusions

  • Flights/train tickets
  • Lunch & personal meals
  • Spa/Ayurveda treatments (optional)
  • Sightseeing transport (if not included)

Why Choose This Tour?

This is a relaxation-first yoga retreat, not a strict program. It’s perfect if you want light yoga combined with a beach holiday.

Best Time to Visit

November to March for pleasant beach weather.
